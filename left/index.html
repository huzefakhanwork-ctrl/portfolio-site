Profile · Lead story

Bangalore strategist closes the gap between thinking and making.

Traded at 17, sold laptops at 18, led 80+ students, launched a brand, then built two agencies. Our reporter followed the numbers.

By the Left Brain · Filed from Bangalore

Huzefa Khan, creative strategist. Brand strategy, content and agency work.

At 17, Huzefa Khan started trading, not to chase money but to understand decisions. A year later, selling laptops at Croma taught him that people buy trust before they buy products.

In college he led large-scale events, managed teams, secured sponsorships, and graduated with the Most Valuable Asset and Best Leader awards. That curiosity led him to build Clenzo, move to Bangalore, start Surreal Factory, and later co-found 3BHK Marketing Agency.

Writing, photography and filmmaking were never just hobbies. They are how he understands the world: a small moment, a strange detail, a passing expression. Somewhere along the way, curiosity stopped being a habit and became the foundation of the work.

His method is plain: every project starts as a question. Why do sponsors say yes? Why does a video actually get watched? Why do small businesses avoid marketing until it's too late? The fastest way to an answer, he says, is to go and do the thing.
